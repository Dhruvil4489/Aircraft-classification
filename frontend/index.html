Supported Defect Taxonomy

The VGG16 classifier operates strictly on exterior aircraft structural panel photographs. Supported damage states include DENT (mechanical depressions from ground equipment impacts, hail, or bird strikes) and CRACK (linear structural fatigue discontinuities caused by cyclic pressurization or vibration).

Out-of-Distribution Rejection Protocol

Standard binary networks forcibly classify arbitrary inputs into one of their two target classes. AeroDamage AI integrates zero-shot contrastive screening to verify domain relevance before running inference. Non-aircraft images (handwritten signatures, documents, portraits, vehicles, landscapes) are safely rejected without emitting misleading defect probabilities.
